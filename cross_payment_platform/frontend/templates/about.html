🌐 What We Do

Cross Payment Platform is an AI-powered payment intelligence system built for Tanzania. It analyses transaction data across {{ platforms|length }} major payment platforms — {{ platforms.keys()|join(', ') }} — to recommend the cheapest, fastest, and most reliable option for a given transaction at any given moment and location.

⚡ How It Works

  1. Enter Transaction Details — amount, type, and location of your planned transfer.
  2. AI Calculates PRI — the engine computes the Payment Reliability Index for every platform using success rate, efficiency, speed, and cost.
  3. Instant Recommendation — you receive the best platform with a fee comparison, estimated speed, and savings amount — all in under a second.

🛡 Our Technology

  • Payment Reliability Index (PRI): 40% Success Rate + 30% Efficiency + 20% Speed + 10% Cost
  • Random Forest classifier for payment success prediction
  • Gradient Boosting model for transaction risk / fraud-pattern flagging
  • Trained on a 20,500-row synthetic Tanzania payment transaction dataset
  • Location-aware routing: recommendations adapt to Tanzania regions
  • Smart routing: transactions above TZS 5,000,000 auto-route to bank/aggregator platforms
  • Peak-hour congestion alerts based on historical traffic patterns

🛡 Privacy Policy

Data Collected: Transaction types, amounts (for savings calculation only), and location names you enter. No personal financial credentials are ever collected.

Data Storage: Your account and history are stored in a local SQLite database on the server you deploy this to.

Data Sharing: We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.

Data Retention: Your data persists until you clear it from Settings → Data Management.

Your Rights: You may access, export, or delete all data held about you at any time via the Settings page.
